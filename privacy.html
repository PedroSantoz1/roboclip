Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

RoboClip is a desktop app that runs entirely on the user's own computer. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and how it can be removed.

1. Information the app accesses

  • Video files you choose. Recordings of your live streams are processed locally on your computer to find moments and create clips. They are not sent to the developer.
  • TikTok account (with your permission, via TikTok Login Kit): your basic profile information (display name, avatar and account ID, scope user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos to your account (scopes video.upload and video.publish).
  • YouTube account (with your permission, via Google sign-in): your channel name and ID, permission to upload videos, and statistics about your own channel and videos (views, subscribers and, if available, revenue).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after you sign in.

2. How the information is used

  • To show which account a clip will be published to.
  • To upload and publish the clips you select, with the caption and privacy setting you choose.
  • To show you statistics about your own published videos, and to improve how the app chooses moments for you (for example, learning which clips performed better on your channels).

The information is used only for these features. It is not used for advertising.

3. Where the information is stored

Everything is stored locally on your computer, in the app's folder: the access tokens, the list of published clips and the statistics. The developer does not run a server that receives your data. The app communicates only with TikTok and Google/YouTube, directly from your computer.

4. Sharing

RoboClip does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform you choose to publish on (TikTok or YouTube), which process them under their own privacy policies: TikTok Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect an account in the app at any time; this deletes its access token from your computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google account (Security → Third-party access).
  • Deleting the app's folder removes all information it stored.

6. Children

RoboClip is not intended for children and does not knowingly process information of anyone under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
